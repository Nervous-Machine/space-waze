Deep dive · Low Earth Orbit · 420–850 km

LEO density — the drag grid.

The question: when the Sun acts up, the upper atmosphere swells and satellites feel more drag. Which solar and geomagnetic drivers move the air in each specific place — and how sure are we?

What we measured.

Air-density response through 40 storms (2021–2025), read from the accelerometers and GPS navigation residuals of two independent satellite fleets that never shared a model: GRACE-FO (460–510 km) and ESA Swarm (420–460 and 510–550 km) — 24 million Swarm measurements across 48 cells, learned one storm at a time with the answer hidden (leave-one-storm-out).

What we found.

Certified

61 storm sectors where the drivers are named.

In a third of the storm sectors we can name the drivers that move the air, with most of that learned weight confirmed by repeated correct prediction — and a deliberately scrambled decoy signal correctly earns nothing there.

Measured

The sky-map replicates.

Where the learner beats simple references is not random — dawn and midnight sectors win, noon does not — and the same pattern appeared on the second fleet, which the model had never seen. Where the learner is not confident, its abstentions correctly flagged its own worst cells before they happened.

Not claimed

What we do not claim.

A head-to-head win over the standard density model (MSIS) once that model gets the same local calibration any operator applies. We tested that claim ourselves and it did not survive — so it is not on the map. What is ours is the driver attribution and the where-the-reference-is-blind map.

Open questions.

gap what closes it

120 cells have no source satellite at all (550–850 km especially) the drag residual any operating satellite already computes for navigation — not telemetry, not proprietary state
severe-storm hours are rare — storm confidence grows in calendar time the next solar-cycle declining-phase season, already scheduled

Receipts.

Every claim on this page comes from a pre-registered analysis: the hypothesis and its pass/fail rule were committed to a ledger before the data was touched, results were reproduced bit-for-bit, decoy signals ran as tripwires, and refused hypotheses stay on record. The complete gap register — data sources, refused hypotheses, and open staked predictions — is published alongside this atlas (how to read it).
